"Whoever he is, he's probably kilt his man in Sage Mound," said the sheriff, "an' then rid over through the butte country, probably ketchin' the train at Painted Bluffs, drappin' off here, figgerin' he had doubled back far enough to fool 'em."

"Yes," said the mayor, licking his dry lips, "but let us postpone guessin' about him until after we has him! I won't feel right until we has him subdued, sheriff."

"We'll git some of the boys," said the sheriff.

Fifteen minutes later, after diligent telephoning on the part of the sheriff, some twenty men had assembled at the sheriff's office, all heavily armed. The sheriff explained—briefly, and the mayor had his say, cautioning care so that there would be no slip-up that might result in shooting and bloodshed.
