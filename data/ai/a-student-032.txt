Policy 2 would not even work. I'm sorry, but it wouldn't. Mr. Daley took eleven phones in one week last October and by Friday every one of those kids had it back in their pocket, because their moms came to the office and asked for them. A ban just means we hide them better. Kids text from inside their hoodie sleeves now.

With Policy 1 there is at least a deal. We get lunch and the ten minutes after the buses drop off, and in return the phone is off, actually off, from first bell on. Most of us would keep that deal since we'd have something to lose. Take lunch phones away from a kid for a week and see how fast he learns.

There is also the bus problem. Route 14 broke down twice this winter. My sister stood outside the middle school for forty minutes in February because nobody could tell my dad the bus wasn't coming, and the office had already locked up. She's eleven. He found her sitting on the salt bin by the side door.
