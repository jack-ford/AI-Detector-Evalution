"the sergeant says your arm's gone bad" i told him. "i only want to look at it"

"the sergeant can go hang. so can you. go on then run and tell him i was uncivil. hell have the irons back on me by supper wont he. thats what you lot like"

"im not telling anybody anything"

he wasnt listening tho. "three weeks i been in this box" he says, "three weeks and every fool in the camp comes and puts his eye to the crack in the door like im a two headed calf at a fair, and now they send a boy with a bag—" and he grabed the tin mug off the floor and threw it at the wall.
