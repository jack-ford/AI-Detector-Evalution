Our principal has decided that every student must take part in at least one extracurricular activity, whether that is a sport, the yearbook, or student council. I agree with this decision. Although some students will complain at first, a requirement like this gives everyone a reason to try something they might never have chosen on their own, and the benefits last well beyond the school year.

The most important benefit is that activities help students find out what they are good at. Classes cover a limited range of subjects, and a student who struggles in math or English may assume he or she is simply not talented. That same student might turn out to be an excellent goalkeeper, photographer, or debater. Without a requirement, many students never discover this because they go straight home after the last bell. One semester on the yearbook staff could reveal an interest in design or writing that eventually leads to a career.
