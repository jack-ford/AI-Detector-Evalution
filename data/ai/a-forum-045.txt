Mostly no. Looking at legal pornography as an adult isn't a crime in most places, and a campus doesn't change that. The question is whether it breaks your institution's rules, and it almost certainly does. Universities nearly all have acceptable use policies for their networks and computers that ban this kind of material, and doing it in a shared office or lab can also count as creating a hostile environment for colleagues, which becomes harassment even if nobody was meant to see it.

So what you risk is a disciplinary process rather than the police: a warning, losing network access, or something worse if you're a graduate student employed by the department. IT staff don't usually watch what you browse, but logs are kept and get checked after a complaint.

Also look up the law in the country you're moving to, since a few criminalize it outright.
