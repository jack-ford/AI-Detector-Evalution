The lock gave on the third try. I eased the hatch shut behind me and stood listening, but the corridor outside stayed quiet, and after a moment I risked the hand torch.

Crates. Forty of them at least, stacked to the overhead and stenciled with the seal of the Ganymede Relief Commission.

I pried the lid off the nearest one. No ration tins, no medical kits. Packed in gray foam lay a dozen slim tubes, each no longer than my forearm, each tipped with a lens of dark crystal.

Needle projectors.

So that was what Varrick had been hauling out to the colonies under a mercy flag. I lifted one free and turned it over, hunting for the charge stud, and that was when the lights in the hold came on.
