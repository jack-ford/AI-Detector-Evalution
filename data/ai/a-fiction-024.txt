Brennan felt his face get hot. He was basically broke and he knew it, but he didn't want this man to know it too. He put his hand over the hole in his glove and hoped that nobody was looking.

"Your a long way from home," the stranger said. He was a big man with a gray beard and he talked loud, like he didn't care who heard.

"I am from Cork, sir," Brennan said. "I came to Paris to study medicine."

"And how is that going for you?"

"Not very well," Brennan admitted. He looked down at the table. The waiter had already came by two times to see if he was going to order anything else, and all he had was one cup of coffee that he finished like an hour ago.

The stranger laughed, but it was not really a mean laugh. "I thought so. You have been staring at that man's soup for ten minutes."

"I was not staring."

"You were. Its alright, I use to do the same thing." He waved his hand at the waiter and said something in French really fast. Then he turned back. "You will eat with me tonight."

"I can't pay you back," Brennan said. "Not right now anyways. My uncle is sending money but it has not came yet."

"Did I ask you to pay?"

"No, but I am not a beggar, sir."

"Nobody said that you were. Sit down and stop being so proud, it doesn't fill your stomach."

Brennan wanted to leave but then the soup came and it smelled so good, so he stayed.
