The Electoral Collage is a system were the people dont vote for the president directly but for electors, this makes it so every state has a say in the desicion. For example: say your school is picking were to go for the feild trip and there is 5 homerooms. Homeroom A is huge it has 40 kids and all of them want the water park. The other 4 homerooms are small and they mostly want the musuem. If you just count every kid then the water park could win only because of one room, and the other 4 rooms opinion basicly dosent matter. But if every homeroom gets it's own votes then the musuem wins since more rooms picked it. This is how the Electoral Collage keeps big states from deciding everthing by themselfs.

Some people disagree and say this is unfair to the voters. "Over 60 percent of voters would prefer a direct election"-source 2. However those voters are not thinking about the small homerooms (states) who would get ignored. Also in source 3 it says candidates "focus their campaign efforts on the toss-up states". Which in the example would be like the teachers only asking the homeroom that hasnt made up there mind yet, that is a good thing because they are the ones really thinking about it and not just picking what there freinds pick.
