It's 7:58 in the morning, your still in pajamas, and your first class starts in two minutes, all you have to do is roll over and open a laptop. Sounds perfect right? For alot of students this is what distance learning looks like, and schools are starting to offer it more and more as an option. Although some people believe that kids can't learn without a teacher standing in front of them, I think that attending classes from home can actually benefit students. Learning from home gives students more control over there own schedule, it takes away many of the distractions that come with a crowded classroom; and it helps students who struggle with anxiety or health problems keep up with there work instead of falling behind.
