The traditional method is feathers and wedges. You drill a line of holes across the rock where you want it to split (a hand star drill and a 3 lb hammer will do it, it's just slow), drop a pair of feathers and a wedge into each hole, and then tap the wedges in sequence, a little at a time, working back and forth along the line. Don't try to drive one wedge all the way home. After a few passes you'll hear the pitch change and the rock will crack along the line.

Wear safety glasses, since chips fly off both the rock and the drill.

If you only need the boulder gone and don't care about the pieces, the lazy option is to dig a pit right beside it, lever it in with a long bar, and cover it over.
