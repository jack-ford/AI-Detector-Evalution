I don't think you really need to do that. Hardening off is for when plants go from inside to outside, because outside is alot harsher with the sun and the wind and cold nights and stuff. Going the other way is basically easier on them. The only thing is that your house has way less light then outside, so the plant might drop some leaves at first and look kind of sad for a couple weeks. Thats normal, its just getting used to it. I would put it by the brightest window you have and check for bugs before you bring it in.
