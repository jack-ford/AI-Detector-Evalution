So the crawler finally got going again. Mace kept both hands on the steering bars and didnt say anything for awhile. The ground out here was all gray dust and rocks, and the treads kept slipping on it. Behind them the lights of the mining dome shrank away.

Relay Four, the chief had said. That was the station that stopped answering two days ago, and nobody knew why.

* * * * *

It was about sixty miles to the relay and most of it was in the dark. Mace drove and Pell watched the dials.

"I bet it's nothing," Pell said. "Probably just a busted antenna or something."

"Then why didn't Lowry come back," Mace said. "He went out there yesterday."

Pell didn't have a answer for that one.

"Hey, look up there," he said a little later. "Is that a light?"

Mace looked. On top of the ridge in front of them there was a small green glow, and it was moving. It went along the rocks real slow and then it stopped, like it knew they were watching. Then all of a sudden it went out, and everything was black again except for the stars.
