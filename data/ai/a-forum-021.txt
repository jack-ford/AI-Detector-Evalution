So basically what I would do is get some of that metal mesh stuff (I think its called hardware cloth) and put it around the edge of the yard where they come in from. You have to bury it a little or they just go under, like maybe 8 inches? I'm not totally sure though.

Also my uncle had this same problem and he said the main thing is too not leave tall grass before winter, because they hide under it when the snow comes. He mowed it really short in like November and it helped alot, but didn't fix it all the way.
