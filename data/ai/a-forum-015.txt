Yes, mostly. You can open a 529 as the account owner without the parents' involvement, and they have no right to be told about it. The catch is that the application asks for each beneficiary's Social Security number and date of birth, which you'd have to get somehow. If that's awkward, open the accounts with yourself as beneficiary for now and switch them to the kids later; changing to another family member is allowed and isn't a taxable event. The money grows the same either way.
