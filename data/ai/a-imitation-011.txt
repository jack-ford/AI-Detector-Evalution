Many drivers believe that they are able to multitask and that looking at their phone for a quick second is not going to cause any harm, however this is the mindset that ends up causing most of the accidents that happen on the road today. When a person is looking at a screen their eyes are off of the road and their mind is somewhere else, so even if a car in front of them brakes they would not have enough time to react to it. Studies have shown that a person who texts while driving is about 23 times more likely to get into a crash then a person who doesn't, which shows how serious of a issue this really is.

Because of this many states have began making laws against using a phone while driving. In Georgia for example there is a hands free law where a driver can not hold the phone in their hand at all, and if they are caught they have to pay a fine and get points on their license. Some people think these laws are too strict since they are adults and should be able to make there own choices. But the choice they are making does not only effect them it effects every other person that is on the road with them, including kids and people who are just walking.
