Driverless cars are often talked about like they are the next big step for society and that soon nobody will ever have to touch a steering wheel again. Companies such as Google and BMW have spent years building cars that can steer, brake, and accelerate by themselves. Supporters say this will make the roads safer for everyone. However, I believe this is the wrong direction to go. Driverless cars should not be developed further because the technology still depends on a human to take over, the laws are not ready for them, and nobody knows who is at fault when an accident happens.

To begin with, the cars that exist today are not truly driverless at all. In the article it explains that these cars can handle driving up to a certain speed, but they alert the driver when there is road work or an accident ahead. This means that the person in the seat must be paying attention the entire time, waiting for their turn to drive. Most people will not be able to stay focused if they are just sitting there doing nothing for an hour. They will get bored and look at their phone or fall asleep, and then when the car needs them they will not be ready to react. A car that is only safe when the human is watching is not much safer then a regular car.

In addition, the laws in most states are not prepared for these vehicles.
