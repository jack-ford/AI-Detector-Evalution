In the name of the Compassionate! From Zuleika to Rashid ibn Harun, peace. The old water-seller beneath your window is in our pay. When he cries his wares three times on the morning of the new moon, be ready; the rope will follow. Horses wait beyond the eastern gate.

The new moon ... three days yet. Rashid chewed the scrap of paper to pulp and swallowed it. Three days of mouldy bread. He had endured worse in Samarkand....

* * * * *

At noon on the appointed day a caravan of salt merchants halted before the citadel, and their leader, a lean man with a dyed beard, begged a word with the Emir.
