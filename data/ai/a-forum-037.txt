ok so easiest way imo is dont try to do it with one size emitter everywhere. the tubing doesnt care, you just punch in diffrent emitters for different plants. tomatoes and squash get the 2 gph ones, sometimes two per plant when its really hot, herbs and peppers get a 0.5 or 1 gph.

if some beds need way more water than others then split it into zones. like a Y splitter at the faucet with a cheap timer on each side, one runs 40 min every morning and the other maybe 15 min every other day. thats what i ended up doing after i drowned my rosemary lol

also get a pressure regulator if you dont have one!! most of this stuff wants like 25 psi and house pressure is way higher, the emitters will pop right off or just spray everywhere.

theres also adjustable emitters you can twist from zero up to like 10gph, handy for pots but honestly they drift and i wouldnt trust them for the whole garden

dont overthink it tho. set it up, run it, then stick your finger in the dirt a few inches down after a couple days. too wet swap for a smaller emiter, too dry add one.
