After dinner Tom went back out to work, and since he had to ride a really long way to check on the fences by the north creek, it was almost dark by the time he got home. While he was gone something happened that Martha did not know what to think about. She was out by the well pulling up water when she seen a rider coming down the hill real slow, like his horse was tired or maybe hurt. At first she thought it was just one of the hired men coming in for supper. But then he got closer and she could tell that it wasn't.

He stopped by the fence and took off his hat. He was older then she expected, with a gray beard and dust all over his coat.

"Is this the Whitlock place?" he asked.

"Yes it is," Martha said. "My husband isn't here right now though."

The man nodded kind of slowly. "I figured he might not be. I'm from the bank over in Clear Forks. I been riding since yesterday morning to get out here, so I hope you can at least give me some coffee."

Martha just stood there holding the bucket. She was glad he couldn't see her hands shaking, because she basically already knew what he came about, and she didn't know if she should let him in the house or not.
