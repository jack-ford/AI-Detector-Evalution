"I bet you anything its the Dawson brothers," the marshal said. He was looking out the window at three horses tied up by the saloon. "Them horses got the same brand as the ones stolen over in Dry Creek."

"Well what are we suppose to do about it," said Mr. Pruitt, who owned the bank and was sweating alot. "The payroll comes in on the noon train and that's in like an hour."

"I know when the train comes, Harold."

The marshal thought about it for a minute. Then he sent the Miller kid to get his deputy and whoever else was around. Pretty soon there was about a dozen men in the back of the feed store, most of them had rifles, and the marshal told them to not shoot unless they had to.
