Drivers who use a phone lose their eyes, their hands and their focus to it all at once, so I think using one in any way should be banned while a vehicle is in motion. In a single recent year, the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration reports, distracted driving killed over 3,000 Americans, and it names texting among the worst distractions. A single message takes roughly five seconds to read, and in that time a car traveling 55 miles per hour covers a football field's length while its driver sees nothing of the road.

Defenders of phone use say hands-free systems fix the problem. They take care of the hands, but the mind stays occupied. Studies show that a driver caught up in a conversation can look right at a red light or a pedestrian and never register it.
