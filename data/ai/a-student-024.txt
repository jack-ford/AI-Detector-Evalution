Another person you can ask is someone in your family, like a older brother or sister or a cousin. They are closer to your age so they kind of get what your going through more then a parent would. For example when I didn't know if I should quit band or not, my sister told me how she quit and regretted it later. That helped me alot because my friends were all just saying do whatever you want.

Coaches are good to ask too. A coach see's you almost everyday and knows how you act under pressure, so their advice is usually pretty honest even if you don't want to hear it.
