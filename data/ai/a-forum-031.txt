Squash ball. That's the detail everyone forgets, and it explains the pulse.

The version Sherlock gives Anderson in The Empty Hearse goes like this. Mycroft's people had a blue inflatable crash bag ready below the roof of Bart's, hidden from John by the low ambulance station building. Sherlock told John on the phone to stay exactly where he was, because from that spot the building blocks the view of the pavement. He jumps, lands on the bag, and the crew drags it away in seconds. Meanwhile Molly has pushed a body out of a window lower down: the dead man Moriarty used as a Sherlock double in the kidnapping. A cyclist knocks John flat, which buys them time to swap the corpse back out so Sherlock can lie down in fake blood, with the squash ball jammed in his armpit to cut off the pulse in his wrist when John grabs it.

Whether that's the true account is another question. Anderson doesn't buy it, and Sherlock walks out grinning.
