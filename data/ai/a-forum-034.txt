Don't tell your current advisor anything until someone else has agreed to take you. I have watched two students in my department do it in the other order, and one of them spent a semester with no funding line and no signature for her annual review.

So: pick the person you would want, go to their office hours, and ask whether they have room and money for a student who is partway through. Say why you're leaving in one sentence and leave the grievances out of it. Faculty talk at lunch.

Once you have a yes, see the graduate program coordinator. There is usually a one-page form, and usually the old advisor doesn't have to sign it. Ask what happens to your data and whether papers already drafted keep his name.

Then tell him in person, briefly, with the decision already made. "I'm moving to Dr. Okafor's group in January" is a statement. He can be angry about it, but there's nothing for him to argue with.
