Every summer students get assigned a project to keep there brains working while school is out. Most of the time the teacher decides what the project is and how to do it. But is that really the best way? If students were the ones who design the projects they would actually want to do them. Students care more about things they pick, they know what they are interested in, and wouldn't wait until the last week of August to start. Summer projects should be student designed because it gives students a reason to care.

When a student picks there own project they put more effort in it. A teacher might assign a book that nobody in the class wants to read and then everyone just looks up the answers online. That is not learning that is just copying. If a student who likes cars got to do a project about how engines work he would probably spend hours on it and not even notice. Students are more creative then teachers think. For example a student could make a video, or build something, or interview there grandparents, instead of writing the same five paragraph essay that every teacher asks for.
