The Face on Mars is nothing more then a mesa, and NASA has the pictures to prove it. When Viking 1 first photographed the region of Cydonia in 1976, scientists said right away that it was a rock formation with shadows that made it look like it had eyes and a mouth. People who believe in aliens say NASA is hiding the truth. However, this does not make sense, because the article points out that finding an ancient civilization on Mars would actually help NASA get more money for its budget. They would have no reason to hide it. In 1998 the Mars Global Surveyor took a photo that was ten times sharper than the original, and it showed no alien monument. Skeptics complained that clouds were in the way, so in 2001 NASA photographed it again on a clear day and the result was the same.
