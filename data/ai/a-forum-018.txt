At that age, physical coins work much better than any explanation. Give her a small, fixed amount before you go into the shop, say three coins, and let her hand them over herself at the till. Once the coins are gone, they are gone, and she can see that with her own eyes.

Keep the choices small. Two items she can afford, and she picks one. If she wants something that costs more, show her how many coins it would take and let her save toward it in a clear jar.

Expect a few tears the first time the money runs out. That is the lesson working, so resist the urge to top it up.
