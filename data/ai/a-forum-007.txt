It's less about any real rule and more about the episode turning Joey's own principle against him. Earlier, he was outraged that Sarah took fries off his plate, and he made a big point of the fact that "Joey doesn't share food." On the second date he works around this by ordering her an extra plate of fries so she'll leave his alone.

Then dessert arrives. Joey's own choice turns out to be a disappointment, because it comes covered in a fruit sauce he wants nothing to do with, while Sarah's chocolate torte looks fantastic. Normally he'd just ask for a bite, but he can't: he has spent the entire evening insisting that people shouldn't eat from each other's plates, and she holds him to it. That's the irony the scene is built on. It's also why he waits until she leaves the table to dig into her dessert, and why he's completely unrepentant when she catches him.
