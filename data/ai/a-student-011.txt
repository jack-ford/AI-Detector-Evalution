A phone takes a driver's eyes, hands and attention away from the road at the same time, which is why I believe drivers should not be allowed to use one in any way while a vehicle is moving. The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration reports that distracted driving killed more than 3,000 people in the United States in a single recent year, and texting is among the most dangerous distractions it tracks. Reading one message takes about five seconds. At 55 miles per hour, a car covers the length of a football field in that time, and the driver sees none of it.

Supporters of phone use argue that hands-free systems solve the problem. They help with the hands, but not with the mind. Researchers have found that drivers absorbed in a conversation can look straight at a red light or a pedestrian and fail to register it.
