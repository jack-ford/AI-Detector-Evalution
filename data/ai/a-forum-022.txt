Honestly I think it depends on where you live, but from what I know in the US they probably don't have to pay you for it. There is a law about this, I think its called the Portal to Portal Act, and it basically says that getting to and from where you actually do your work doesn't count as work time. Even if the shuttle is mandatory. Which seems kind of unfair to me but that is how it is.

So like if the shuttle takes 30 minutes each way, thats an hour a day your not getting paid for, and I get why your mad about that. I would be too.

What I would do is just ask your manager or HR about it in a nice way. Don't go in there saying they are breaking the law because they probably aren't. Maybe they will let you clock in when you get on the shuttle, you never know until you ask.
