short answer no. longer answer: on paper yeah the math can flip, if a ticket is $2 and odds are 1 in 292 million then once the jackpot goes past like 600 mil the "expected value" looks positive. people post this every time powerball gets huge

but it falls apart fast. first off the advertised number is the 30 yr annuity, the cash option is way less. then taxes eat another third or more. and the bigger the jackpot gets the more ppl buy tickets so the odds of splitting it go way up, and splitting it in half kills your EV right there

and even if it WAS positive ev its still not an investment. you'd have to play millions of times for the average to show up and you get one shot, 99.99999% of the time your 2 dollars is just gone. thats a bet not a portfolio

buy one for fun if you want, i do sometimes. just dont call it investing and definately dont put rent money in
