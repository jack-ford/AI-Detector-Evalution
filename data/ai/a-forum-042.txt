it'll sorta work but not nearly as good as you think. problem is a single hose portable is blowing room air outside thru that hose, like a lot of it, and that air has to get replaced from somewhere. so the room pulls in air from wherever it can, under the door, gaps round the window, the attic hatch, and all of thats warm air from outside or the rest of the house. so your basically cooling the room and sucking heat back in at the same time.

if your venting it up near the ceiling and theres any opening up there it'll probly just pull the same hot air straight back down

what you want is a dual hose unit, or check if yours has a spot for a second hose on the back (some do, theres a grille you can pop an adapter on). one hose brings outside air in to cool the hot side, other hose dumps it back out. then the room air just stays in the room going round and round getting colder
