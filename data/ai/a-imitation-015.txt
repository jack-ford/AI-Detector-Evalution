In the story Luke Bomberger had a job at a store and a bank but then his frend Don asked him to go on a cattel boat. Luke new this was a chance he wood not get agin so he said yes. I think he made a good choise.

The boat was takeing horses and cows to countrys that got reckt in the war. Luke had to feed them and give them water two or three times evry day. That is a lot of work for one kid. One night it was raining and he sliped down a lader and almost went in the ocen. He hurt his ribs and coudnt work for a few days.

But it wasnt all bad. Luke got to see Greece and China and he even rode a gondala in Venice wich is a city with water for streets. On the way back the animels were gone so the cowboys playd baseball and volyball where the stalls use to be.

Luke went on nine trips wich was more then any body. He says it made him care about other peple more, and that is why he thinks you shoud be a seagoing cowboy to.
