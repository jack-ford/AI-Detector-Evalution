Summer is the time of year that every student looks forward too, but then the teacher hands out a summer project and it kind of ruins it. Most schools give these projects so that kids dont forget everything they learned. The big question is who should come up with the project, the teacher or the student. I think that summer projects should be teacher-designed because teachers know what we need to learn, it is more fair for everyone, and students would probably just pick something easy.

The first reason summer projects should be teacher-designed is that teachers know what is coming up next year and we dont. For example, if I am going into chemistry next year I have no idea what I am suppose to know before I get there. My teacher does though. She could give us a project about the periodic table or something like that, so when we walk in on the first day we are not completely lost. If I made my own project I would probably do it on something I already know about, which doesnt really help me. Also teachers have been doing this for a long time, so they know what kind of projects actually work and which ones are just a waste of time. This is why having teachers design it makes the most sense.
