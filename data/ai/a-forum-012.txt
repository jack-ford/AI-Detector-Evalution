I'd respect the invitation. When hosts write "no presents" they usually mean it, and there is often a reason behind it: they may not want other families to feel obliged to spend money, or they simply have a small house and a child who already owns plenty. If one guest shows up with a wrapped gift, the children who followed the instructions can feel awkward, and so can their parents.

That doesn't mean your son's impulse should be shut down. Wanting to give something to a friend is a lovely instinct. A handmade card or a drawing is almost always welcome and doesn't really count as a present in the sense the hosts meant. If he has his heart set on a particular gift, he could give it to his friend privately on another day, away from the party.
