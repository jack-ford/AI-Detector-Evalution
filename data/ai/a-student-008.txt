I am writing about the two cell phone policies you are considering for next year. After thinking about both, I believe Policy 1, which lets students bring phones and use them during lunch and free periods as long as they are off during class, is the better choice.

The strongest argument for Policy 1 is communication. Plans change during the school day. Practices get canceled, rides fall through, and parents need to reach their children without calling the front office and waiting for a message to be delivered. A phone that stays silent in a backpack during class and comes out at lunch solves that problem without interrupting a single lesson. A total ban would take that option away from every family, including the ones whose children have never misused a phone.
