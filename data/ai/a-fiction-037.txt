the ship was a barque, wooden, nothing fancy. some grain merchant in his home town had her built, first proper deep water ship that port ever had apparantly and the whole place turned out for the launch, band and flags and the merchants wife smashing the bottle. Brandt said farmers walked in from the villages just to stand on the quay and look at her. he was second mate. twenty four years old and he thought he was made for life, you could tell he still half thought so, and his people were decent people back there, his father had been the schoolmaster

the merchant was the ambitious type. nobody from up there had ever sent a ship round the Horn before, they did the baltic and maybe england and that was it. but he filled her with cement and iron rails and off she went to try her luck. Valparaiso first i think he said. or callao, i forget
