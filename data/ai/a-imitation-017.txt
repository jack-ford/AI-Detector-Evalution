I think the school should go with the second policy and not let students have phones at school at all. The biggest reason is that phones cause a lot of drama. Last year two girls in my grade got into a huge fight because of something that was posted during lunch, and by sixth period everybody had seen it. If nobody had a phone out that would of never happened. Phones also get stolen or broken at school. Then the student blames the school and the parents get mad, even though it was the students choice to bring it. Another reason is that kids don't really talk to each other anymore when they have phones. At lunch you would see a whole table of people just staring at there screens and not saying anything, that is not what lunch is for. Some people say they need it for emergencies but a real emergency does not happen very often. That is why I think the second policy is better for everyone.
