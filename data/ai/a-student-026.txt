Almost everybody has a cell phone now, and most people have it on them pretty much all the time. This is fine when your at home or at school but it becomes a big problem when people are behind the wheel of a car. Everyday people get into accidents because they were looking down at a text or changing a song instead of watching the road. In my opinion drivers should not be allowed to use their phones at all while driving, because it is dangerous for them and for everyone else on the road.

The first reason is that using your phone takes your attention off of driving. When you are driving you need to be looking at the road, the other cars, stop signs, people walking, and a lot of other things all at once. If you look down at your phone for even five seconds, that is five seconds where you basically have no idea what is happening in front of you. I read that at 55 miles per hour a car goes about the length of a football field in that amount of time, which is crazy to think about. A kid could run out into the street or the car in front of you could slam on there brakes and you wouldn't even know until it was too late. Some people say that they are good at multitasking and that they can text and drive fine, but I don't think that is true. My cousin thought that too and she ended up rear ending somebody at a red light last year. Nobody got hurt luckily but her car was messed up and her insurance went way up.
