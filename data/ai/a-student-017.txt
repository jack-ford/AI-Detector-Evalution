Nearly every student at our school carries a cell phone, and no rule is going to change that. The real question is what we are allowed to do with them once we walk through the doors. Of the two policies being considered, I believe the better one is to let students bring phones and use them during lunch and other free periods, as long as they stay off during class.

The strongest reason is safety and simple logistics. Practices get cancelled, buses run late, and parents' work schedules change in the middle of the day. When a student can send a quick text at lunch, those problems are solved in thirty seconds. Under a total ban, the same student has to ask for a pass, wait in line at the office phone, and hope a parent picks up a call from an unfamiliar number. The office staff have enough to do without becoming a message service for nine hundred students.

A full ban is also very hard to enforce. Teachers would spend time searching for phones that are hidden in pockets and backpacks instead of teaching. A rule that students see as reasonable is one that most of them will actually follow, and limiting phones to free time is a rule like that.
