I live in Florence, so I can only speak for what I see here. Day to day, very little has changed. Shops, restaurants and museums are open, trains are running on schedule, and the main difference is that the centre is quieter than it normally is at this time of year because a lot of tour groups have cancelled.

Tuscany has had a handful of confirmed cases, all linked to people who had recently been in the affected areas further north. The towns under quarantine are in Lombardy and Veneto, several hours away, and you would not be allowed into them even if you wanted to go.

The practical risk for a traveller right now is less about catching the virus and more about disruption. Flights are being cancelled at short notice, some countries are asking people returning from Italy to self-isolate, and the rules could change while you are here. Check what your airline and your travel insurance will cover before you decide.
