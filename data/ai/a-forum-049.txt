I'd keep the cooking out of it. Dry heat is fine for wood, but steam from a rice cooker or a pot of boiling water keeps soaking into the grain, and within a few weeks you'll see warping, lifting veneer and likely mold in the corners. Foil only helps if every seam is sealed, and it leaves the larger problem untouched: a hot plate inside a closed box of bare timber has nowhere to release its heat.

Use the cabinet to store things. Cook on a tile or a metal tray in the open, close to a window, and put everything away once it has cooled and dried.
