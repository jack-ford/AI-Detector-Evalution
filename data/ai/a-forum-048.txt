Work out what the item is worth to you before asking its price, and treat that figure as fixed. The rest is theater. With no ceiling of your own, the seller's first number becomes your anchor, and you'll walk away pleased with yourself for paying half of something that was tripled from the start.

Spend ten minutes browsing first. Ask at two or three stalls with the same item, committing to nothing, and you'll learn roughly where prices really land. Then open well below your ceiling, stay friendly, and raise your offer in small steps. Sellers expect this and don't take it personally.

Your one real source of leverage is being willing to leave, so be ready to do it. If the vendor calls after you, you were above their floor. If not, you've lost nothing, and another stall is usually just around the corner.
