Ok so this happened with my little brother when he was like 10 months old. He loved baths and then one day he just started screaming every time, and my mom couldnt figure out why. It turned out the water was a little too hot one time and it scared him, so after that he thought every bath was going to be like that.

Babys remember stuff like that more then you would think. It could also be that he slipped, or got soap in his eyes, or even just that the drain made a loud noise. You might not have even noticed when it happened.

What worked for us was not forcing it. My mom stopped doing real baths for about a week and just wiped him down with a washcloth. Then she put him in the tub with no water and some toys so he could play. After that she added a little bit of water each time. It took a while but he was fine again, so I would try that.
