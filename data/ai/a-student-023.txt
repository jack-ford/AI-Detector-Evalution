Another reason limiting car usage is good is because it helps with pollution. In the article about Paris, it says the smog got so bad that they had to ban half the cars from driving for a day, and the traffic went down by 60 percent (Duffer). So if one city can do that in just a couple days imagine what would happen if people did it all the time.

Also people are actually happier without cars. In Vauban, Germany most of the familys dont even own a car and they like it that way (Rosenthal). They can walk to the store and kids can play outside without worrying about getting hit.

In conclusion, limiting car usage has a lot of advantages. It makes the air cleaner and it makes people less stressed out, and I think more citys should try it.
