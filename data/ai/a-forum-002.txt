Decide what the item is worth to you before you ask the price, and treat that number as fixed. Everything else is theater. If you don't have a ceiling in mind, the seller's opening figure becomes your reference point, and you'll end up feeling clever for paying half of something that was tripled to begin with.

It helps to spend ten minutes looking first. Ask at two or three stalls selling the same thing without committing, and you'll get a rough sense of where prices actually settle. Then make an offer well under your ceiling, stay friendly, and move up in small steps. Sellers expect this and don't take it personally.

Walking away is your only real leverage, so be prepared to do it. If the vendor calls you back, you were above their floor. If they don't, you've lost nothing, and there's usually another stall around the corner.
