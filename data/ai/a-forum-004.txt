In most countries, no: for an adult, viewing legal pornography is not a criminal offence, and being on a university campus doesn't change that. The real question is whether it breaks your institution's rules, and it almost certainly does. Nearly every university has an acceptable use policy for its network and computers that prohibits this kind of content, and doing it in a shared office or lab can additionally be treated as creating a hostile environment for colleagues, which moves it into harassment territory even if nobody was meant to see it.

So the realistic risk is not the police but a disciplinary process: a warning, loss of network access, or, for a graduate student employed by the department, something more serious. IT staff generally aren't watching what you browse, but logs exist and get examined when someone complains.

Check the laws of the country you're moving to as well, since a few do criminalize it outright.
