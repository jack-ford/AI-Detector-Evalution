"ok fine" said the two big ducklings and off they went down to the pond, but Dibby the littlest one didnt want to go he wanted to stay by his mama. so Mrs Waddle gave him a spoon and a old tin pan to bang on and he sat under the table happy as anything, while her and Mrs Puddlefoot got started shelling the peas.

and they were having the nicest chat, all about the new fence and who's eggs hatched late this year, when there was this AWFUL noise from outside. clang clang clang and then somebody yelling.

"what on earth" said Mrs Puddlefoot and they both ran out to the yard so fast the peas went everywhere.

one of her ducklings was coming up the path flapping like crazy. "mama mama come quick its Pip, he, he" and then she ran out of air and just stood there going huh huh huh.

"he what. spit it out child" Mrs Waddle said, kinda snappy bc she was scared.

"he's got the bucket on him! the milk bucket! and it wont come off and he cant see and he keeps walking into the gate!"

so they hurried round the barn and yep. there was Pip. or the bottom half of Pip anyway, two orange feet and a tail sticking out from under the bucket, going round and round in a circle and bonking into the gate post evry time, clang.

"Pip Puddlefoot how many times have i told you to keep your head out of things that arent yours!!" his mother hollered. "now how do you think your getting out of there"

"mmf mmf mmf" said the bucket.

well Mrs Waddle tried to keep a straight face she really did but she couldnt, she laughed till she had to sit down on the chopping block. "oh dont scold him Polly" she said wiping her eyes, "i think he's learnt it allready"

and honestly if youd been there youd of laughed too
