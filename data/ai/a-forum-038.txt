yeah thats normal. when you hang horizontal the top sheet goes up first tight to the ceiling and the bottom one gets cut to fit, so the cut edge ends up down at the floor. no taper on a cut edge = nothing to fill. plus theres a half inch gap down there anyway so the board isnt sitting on the slab

even if the factory edge did land at the bottom its like 2 inches of taper and your baseboard is gonna cover it. baseboard gets nailed up higher into the plate/studs so it doesnt care. i've hung prob a few hundred rooms and never once seen a taper mud the floor line, total waste of mud

only time id bother is if your doing real skinny trim or no base at all (some modern houses do that reveal thing) then yeah tell the finsher ahead of time
