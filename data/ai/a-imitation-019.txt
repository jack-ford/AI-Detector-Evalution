Attending classes from home would be a benefit to students because it allows them to work at a pace that fits them, gives them more time for sleep and jobs, and keeps them from falling behind when they can not make it to school. Some people argue that, students at home will get distracted and not do there work without a teacher watching them.

To begin, students would be able to work at their own pace when taking classes from home. In a normal classroom the teacher has to move on when the bell rings, even if half of the class did not understand the lesson. For instance, a student who struggles in math may need to hear the directions two or three times before it makes sense to them. With online classes, that student is able to pause or rewatch a video until they understand it.
