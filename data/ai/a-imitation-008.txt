The use of technolgy to read student emotions in a classroom is valuable for several reasons. Computers that can read faces would be able to notice when a student is bored or lost. They could also help the students who are to shy to speak up. Finally it could save time for everyone invovled. Catching boredom, helping quiet students, and saving time are the reasons why the Facial Action Coding System belongs in schools.

To begin, a computer that reads expressions can tell when a lesson is not working. In the article Dr. Huang explains that a classroom computer could recognize when a student is getting confused or bored and then change the lesson, like a human instructor would. This is importnt because most online lessons today are the same for every person no mater how they are reacting to it. If a student has been staring at the same paragraph with a blank look for five minutes, the progam could switch to a video or give an easier example. Without this the student would most likley just give up and click through.

In addition this technology would help students who do not like asking for help. Many students are afraid of looking dumb infront of their classmates so they stay quiet even when they have no idea what is going on. Their face still shows it though. The article says the software tracks the movment of muscles in the face, such as the ones that raise your eyebrows when you are suprised, so a student can not really hide confusion from it. Because of this the teacher would know to check on that student with out the student having to raise there hand.
