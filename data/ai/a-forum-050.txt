For a child that young, real coins teach better than any explanation. Before you enter the shop, give her a small fixed amount, three coins, say, and have her hand them over herself at the till. When the coins have gone, they have gone, and she can see it for herself.

Keep her choices few: two things she can afford, and she picks one. If she wants something pricier, show how many coins it would cost and let her save toward it in a clear jar.

The first time the money runs out, expect some tears. That's the lesson working, so don't give in and top her up.
