Have you ever had to make a choice and had no idea what to do? It happens to everyone at some point. Maybe it is something small like which shoes to buy, or something big like which high school to go to. When people are stuck, they usually ask someone else what they think. Asking more than one person is even better, and there are a few reasons why.

First of all, one person only knows so much. If you ask just your best friend, you are only getting one side of things. Your friend might tell you to join the soccer team because she likes soccer, not because it is right for you. But if you ask three or four people, you start to hear different ideas. One person might bring up something that nobody else thought of. Then you can put all of those ideas together and see the whole picture, instead of just a peice of it.

Another reason is that people can be wrong. Even adults make mistakes when they give advice. Last year I asked my uncle if I should take the harder math class, he said no because it would be too much work. Then I asked my teacher and my mom, and they both said I could handle it. I took the class and ended up with a B. If I had only listened to my uncle, I would of missed out. When most of the people you ask say the same thing, it is probably good advice.

Some people might say that asking a lot of people just makes you more confused. That can be true sometimes, but I think it is still worth it. You don't have to do what everybody says. You are just collecting opinions, and at the end you are the one who gets to decide.

So the next time you have a tough choice to make, don't just ask one person and stop there. Talk to a few different people you trust. It might take a little longer, but your decision will probally be a better one.
