I wouldn't cook inside it. Wood handles dry warmth reasonably well, but repeated steam from a rice cooker or a pot of boiling water will get into the grain, and over a few weeks you'll see warping, lifted veneer and probably mold in the corners. Lining it with foil only helps if every seam is sealed, and it does nothing about the bigger problem: a hot plate in a closed box of bare timber has nowhere to shed heat.

Use the cabinet for storage instead. Do the actual cooking on a tile or a metal tray out in the open, near a window, and put things away once they've cooled and dried.
