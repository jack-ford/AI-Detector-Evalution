I am writing to you about the idea of making students have a B average to be in sports or other activities. I don't really agree with this and I think alot of other students feel the same way. For some kids, sports is the only reason they even want to come to school. If you take that away from them because they have a C in math then they are probably not going to try harder, they are just going to stop caring at all. My friend is on the basketball team and he works really hard in science but it is just a hard class for him and he gets C's. He is not lazy or anything. I think a better idea would be to have like a study hall before practice for the kids who's grades are low, so that way they can get help and still be on the team.
