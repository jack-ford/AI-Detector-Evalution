It's the bracelet scam. They work the steps below Sacré-Cœur mostly, the bottom of the funicular, sometimes the bridges near Notre-Dame. One of them gets a loop of coloured thread round your finger or wrist while chatting about where you're from, and starts braiding. Takes maybe forty seconds. Now it's knotted on, you can't pull it off, and he wants ten or twenty euros for it. His friends drift over while you argue.

Got my wife in 2011. She paid five to make it stop.

What works is keeping your hands in your pockets and not slowing down. Don't answer the "hello my friend, Africa, hakuna matata" stuff, don't shake a hand. If someone takes hold of your arm, pull it back hard and say "Lâchez-moi" loudly enough that people look round. They won't chase you. There are forty other tourists coming up behind.
