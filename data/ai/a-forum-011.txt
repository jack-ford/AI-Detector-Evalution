Yes, people camp in tick country all the time; you just need a routine. Treat your clothes and socks with permethrin a day or two before the trip, and let them dry completely. Wear long pants tucked into your socks, in a light color so a crawling tick shows up. Use a repellent with DEET or picaridin on exposed skin.

Every evening, do a full-body check, including the scalp, behind the knees and along the waistband. If you find one attached, pull it straight out with fine-tipped tweezers, gripping close to the skin. A tick has to feed for a day or more to pass on Lyme, so daily checks matter most.
