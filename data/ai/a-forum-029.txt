Gregor held Sandor's face against the coals of a brazier when they were boys, over a toy, and the burned half never healed right. He has been afraid of flame ever since. At the Blackwater Tyrion sets the whole bay alight with wildfire, and Sandor is sent out through the Mud Gate into it. Men are burning in front of him. One runs at him on fire. He comes back inside white-faced and refuses to lead another sortie.

But he could have just hidden in a cellar until morning, and he doesn't. He tells Joffrey to his face what he can do with his Kingsguard and his city. Sandor never took a knight's vows. He despised the boy, and he had watched him have Sansa beaten in the throne room. Dying in green fire for that king was a bad trade. So he drank, went to Sansa's room and offered to take her north. She said no. He rode out alone.
