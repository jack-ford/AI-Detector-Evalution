Thomas Avery was walking back from the mill that night and it was pretty late, probably around ten or so. The road went along by the creek and there was a lot of fog coming off the water so he couldn't really see that far ahead of him. He was thinking about the letter he got from his sister that morning, so he wasn't paying much attention to anything.

Then he heard something. It was like a stick breaking, somewhere back behind him in the trees. He stopped and listened for a second but there was nothing, so he figured it was just a deer or a raccoon and he kept going.

But then he heard it again and this time he knew it wasn't an animal because it stopped right when he stopped. Somebody was following him. Thomas got off the road real quick and stood behind the old fence post by the bridge and waited, his heart was beating kind of fast now.

After a minute a small shape came out of the fog. It was a boy. He was barefoot and had on a coat that was way to big for him, and when he got closer Thomas could see it was Eli, the youngest one of the Parker kids from up the hollow.

Thomas was a little annoyed at first, since he just wanted to get home. But he always felt sorry for Eli, and also he wondered what a kid that age was doing out here by himself. So he stepped out and said, "Eli? What are you doing way out here, does your ma know your gone?"

The boy jumped and looked like he was about to run off. Then he didn't.

"Mister Avery I got to tell you something," he said, and his voice was shaking. "I ain't supposed to. But I seen what they did with your horses and it ain't right."
