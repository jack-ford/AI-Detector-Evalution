Feathers and wedges is the old-fashioned method. Drill a row of holes along the line where you want the rock to break (a hand star drill and a 3 lb hammer will do, though it's slow), seat a pair of feathers and a wedge in each, and strike the wedges one after another, a little at a time, working along the row and back. Don't drive any single wedge all the way in. After a few passes the sound of the hammer changes, and the rock cracks along your line.

Wear safety glasses, because chips fly off both the rock and the drill.

If you just want the boulder gone and don't care what shape it's in, the lazy way is to dig a hole beside it, lever it in with a long bar, and bury it.
