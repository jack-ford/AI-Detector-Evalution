Living without a car also changes how people spend their money. The average driver pays for gas, insurance, repairs and parking every month, and those costs add up to thousands of dollars a year. In a community designed around walking and public transit, that money can go toward rent, food or savings instead. Residents of Vauban, Germany, where most streets are closed to cars, report feeling less stressed than they did when they depended on driving.

Health is another benefit. People who walk or bike to work and school get regular exercise without having to set aside time for it, which lowers the risk of obesity and heart disease.
