I know that a lot of students are going to be upset about this, but I think changing the policy so that you need a B average to play sports is actually a good idea. School is suppose to come first. If a student is spending three hours at practice every night and then failing their classes, something is wrong and somebody needs to step in. A rule like this would help students stay focused, it would teach them to manage there time, and it would make our teams look better too.

First, it would help students stay focused on what matters. Most of us are not going to play in the NBA or the NFL, even if we think we are. We are going to need to get into college and get a job. My older brother played football all four years and barley passed his classes, and now he tells me all the time that he wishes someone had made him study. Nobody did so he didn't.

Second, students would learn how to manage their time. When I was on the swim team last year I had practice untill six and then homework after. At first I was so tired I just went to bed, and my science grade dropped to a C. My mom told me I had to quit if didn't bring it up. So I started doing homework on the bus and at lunch and it went back to a B in about a month. I wouldnt have done that if she hadn't of pushed me.
