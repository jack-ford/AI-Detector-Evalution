I think you should sign up to be a Seagoing Cowboy like I did. It is a job were you take horses and cows on a boat to countrys that got hurt in World War II. My freind Don asked me to come and I said yes right away.

The best part was all the places I went to. I crossed the Atlantic Ocean 16 times! I saw a castle in Crete and I went to the Panama Canal on the way to China.

It was alot of work to. One night I sliped on a ladder and craked my ribs. But on the way home we played baseball and volleyball were the animals use to be. It was fun.
