Have you ever had to make a big decision and you didn't know what to pick? Maybe it was what sport to try out for or if you should tell your friend something that might make them mad. When that happens to me I don't just ask one person, I ask a bunch of people. I believe that getting more than one opinion helps you make a better choice because everybody sees things in a different way.

One person only knows what they have been through. Say you ask your older brother if you should take the hard math class and he says no because he hated it. That's just him though. If you also asked your teacher and a friend who took it last year, they might tell you it wasn't that bad and that it helps you later on. Now you have three opinions instead of one and you can see the whole picture. If i only listened to my brother I would of missed out.

Another thing is that people can be biased. Your best friend might tell you what you want to hear so that you won't get upset with them. That feels nice but it doesn't really help you. Somebody who isn't as close to you, like a coach or a counselor, will probably be more honest about it. When you put those answers next to each other you can tell which ones are real advice and which ones are just being nice.

Also, when a lot of people say the same thing it makes you feel more sure. If four out of five people tell you to apologize to your friend then you probably should. And if they all say something different, at least you know its a hard question and you should take your time on it.
