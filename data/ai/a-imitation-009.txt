I do not think the principal should change the rule to a B average. A lot of kids at our school have a C and they still work hard. If you take sports away from them then they have nothing to look forward to. Some kids only come to school because of basketball or band. That is the truth. i know a few of them.

Having a C does not mean you are lazy. Some classes are just hard for people. My friend studies every night for math and still gets a C on the tests. He is the best runner on our track team. If the rule changes he is off the team and that isn't fair to him or to us. We would lose meets because of it.

Also sports help you with school. You learn to show up on time and listen to the coach. Kids who get kicked off will just go home and play video games, they wont study more. Then there grades could even go down. The school should help C students with tutoring instead of punishing them. That way everybody gets better and we still have good teams.
