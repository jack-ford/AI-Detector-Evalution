"thats the lot of em i think. no wait. Ezra get down off that roof and let the man see you"

a boy slid down the shed and landed in the mud and grinned at me. i wrote him down. that made seven and my form only had lines for six so i started going up the margin.

"and your husband ma'am?" i said

"down the quarry. him and the two big ones, they dont get home till dark"

"two more?"

"well yes" she said like i was slow, "Amos and John."

i just wrote. nine children, one cow, a house with i think two rooms and a stove that was smoking so bad my eyes were running. or thats what i told myself it was. she was so thin you coud see the bones in her wrists when she pushed her hair back.

"how do you feed them all Mrs Tolliver" i said and right away wished i hadnt, its not on the form.

she looked at me a second. "Ezra take the baby in. go on all of you, git." then when they were gone, "we feed em mister. some weeks better then others. the quarry pays the first of the month if it pays"
