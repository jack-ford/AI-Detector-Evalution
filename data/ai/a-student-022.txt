Cars are a huge part of peoples lives today, but that doesn't mean they are always a good thing. A lot of cities and countries are starting to realize that having less cars around is actually better for everybody. There are many advantages to limiting car usage, such as less pollution in the air, less stress for people, and also saving a lot of money. If more people would cut back on driving I think the world would be a better place to live in.

To start off, limiting car usage is really good for the environment. Cars put out a lot of gases that are bad for the air and this causes things like smog. In the article about Paris, it says that the smog got so bad that the city had to ban half of the cars from driving for a few days. After they did that the smog went down enough that they could lift the ban. This shows that cars were a big reason for the pollution in the first place. If one city can clear up it's air in just a couple days, imagine what would happen if everyone drove less all the time.

Another advantage is that people are less stressed out when they don't have to drive everywhere. Driving can be very stressful because of traffic, and other drivers, and trying to find parking. In Vauban, Germany, most people there don't even own a car, and one mom said that she is much happier now then when she had one. I can understand why she feels that way. When you walk or ride a bike you get to be outside and get some exercise, instead of just sitting in traffic being mad. Also in Bogota, Colombia they have a day every year with no cars and millions of people walk or bike to work, and they said it was a good way to take away stress.

Lastly, not driving as much can save you a ton of money.
