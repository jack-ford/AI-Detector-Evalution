My cousin Dana did tenth grade from her kitchen table. She has Crohn's, and that year she missed forty-one days before October was over, so her school let her switch to online classes. She logged in at nine, sometimes ten. She passed everything. I think about her whenever people say kids who learn from home are just being lazy, because for her it was the only way to stay a sophomore.

Schools should let students take classes from home if they want to. Not every student. Some kids I know would sleep until noon and fail by Thanksgiving, and they would tell you so themselves.

But start with sleep. Our first bell rings at 7:20. The bus comes to my street at 6:15, which means I am up at 5:30, in the dark, eating cereal I can't taste. By third period half the room has their heads down on the desk.
