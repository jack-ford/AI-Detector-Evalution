The Electoral College should be changed to election by popular vote. Even though it has been around since the founding fathers, it is not the best way to pick the President anymore. First of all, the winner of the popular vote can still lose the election. Second, canidates only pay attention to a few states. Last, the electors do not even have to vote for who there state picked. For these reasons the Electoral College needs to go.

The biggest problem is that the person with the most votes does not always win. This happend in 2000 when Al Gore got more votes then George W. Bush across the country but Bush still became President because of the electoral votes. According to the article, "over 60 percent of voters would prefer a direct election to the kind we have now." If most of the people want one person and they get the other one, then how is that a democrocy. It makes people feel like there vote did not count for anything.

Canidates only care about the swing states. Because almost every state gives all of its electoral votes to whoever wins that state, the canidates dont bother going to states they know they will win or lose. The article says that in 2000 seventeen states did not see the canidates at all. So if you live in a state like Texas or California your basically ignored, while Ohio and Florida get all of the attention and the campaign ads.

Finally, the electors are not always loyal. When you vote you are not really voting for the President, you are voting for a group of electors who promise to vote for him. But some of them have broke that promise before, they are called "faithless electors". It does not happen alot but the fact that it can happen at all is a problem. The people should be the ones who decide and not a few electors that nobody has heard of.

In conclusion our country should get rid of the Electoral College!
